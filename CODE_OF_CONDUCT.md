# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participate with respect, curiosity, and care. Welcome constructive disagreement about product,
security, and design decisions. Critique work and evidence rather than people. Harassment,
discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, and deliberate disruption are unacceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This standard applies in the repository and project-managed community spaces. Report conduct issues
privately to the repository maintainer through GitHub. Do not include sensitive personal details in
a public issue. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, limit participation, or ban accounts
when necessary to protect the community.

Maintainers will handle reports as privately as practical, avoid conflicts of interest, and consider
context, severity, repeated behavior, and community safety when choosing a response.
